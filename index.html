Getting started

  1. Upload or record a voice sample you have permission to use.
  2. Give the sample a name and save it in your Voices library.
  3. Enter the words you want spoken.
  4. Generate the audio once the voice engine is connected.

Chip usage

Each character in your script—including spaces and punctuation—costs one chip. Your starting balance is 100,000 chips. This interface currently previews the cost; generation is not connected yet.

Privacy

Saved voice samples are kept in this browser on this device. Avoid using shared devices for private recordings.
